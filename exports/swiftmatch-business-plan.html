01 · EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Executive Summary

SwiftMatch is an assessment-first recruitment platform built for the Philippine labor market. We flip the hiring model on its head: instead of applicants endlessly searching and spraying résumés, verified talent gets spotted by employers based on objective, multi-dimensional evidence of capability.

Traditional job boards optimize for volume, not fit. Employers drown in unqualified applications; qualified candidates are filtered out by keyword-matching software before a human ever reads their story. SwiftMatch replaces the résumé-keyword lottery with a structured evaluation engine that scores every candidate across five dimensions — Knowledge & Expertise, Personality & Work Style, Cultural Fit, Critical Thinking, and AI Readiness — across 22 industries including the fast-growing Virtual Assistance sector.

The Opportunity

The Philippines has a workforce of ~49 million, one of the world's largest English-speaking talent pools, a globally dominant BPO industry (~1.7M employed), and an explosively growing remote / virtual-assistance economy serving US, AU, and UK clients. Yet hiring infrastructure remains stuck on résumé-keyword job boards. SwiftMatch is positioned to become the trusted, skills-verified layer between this talent and the employers who need it.

Business Model

SwiftMatch operates a freemium two-sided model. Applicants build a verified profile and take assessments for free. A Premium tier unlocks the full candidate analytics report, career-transition recommendations, and priority visibility. Employers pay for access to the ranked candidate pool, advanced matching, custom per-job assessments, and a hiring pipeline with built-in pass/fail decisioning and candidate notifications.

The ask: SwiftMatch is raising a pre-seed round to convert a live MVP into a revenue-generating platform — funding go-to-market, employer sales, assessment-science validation, and email/notification infrastructure across the first 18 months.

03 · THE PROBLEM

The Problem

Hiring in the Philippines is high-volume, low-signal, and exhausting for both sides of the market.

For Job Seekers

  • The application black hole. Candidates submit dozens of applications and hear nothing. Résumé-keyword filters reject qualified people before a human reviews them.
  • No way to prove real skill. A self-written résumé can't demonstrate critical thinking, cultural fit, or AI fluency. Strong candidates with weak résumé-writing skills lose out.
  • Wasted effort. The same person re-enters the same data into ten different portals, tailors ten résumés, and still competes on presentation rather than ability.

For Employers

  • Application overload. A single posting attracts hundreds of unqualified applicants; screening is manual and slow.
  • Poor signal. Résumés don't predict on-the-job performance, retention, or team fit — leading to costly mis-hires.
  • No standardized comparison. Two candidates can't be objectively compared because there's no common measurement.
  • AI blind spot. As AI reshapes every role, employers have no way to measure a candidate's actual AI readiness.

The core gap: The market has plenty of job boards (places to post and apply) but no trusted measurement layer (a way to objectively know who is actually qualified and a good fit). SwiftMatch is that layer.

04 · THE SOLUTION

The Solution

SwiftMatch turns every candidate into a structured, comparable, verified data profile — and surfaces the best-fit people to employers automatically.

How It Works

  1. Build once. Candidates complete a guided 10-step profile (with résumé auto-fill via AI) capturing skills, history, references, preferences, and an intro video.
  2. Get measured. Candidates take industry-scoped assessments across five dimensions. Questions are randomized and graded server-side for integrity.
  3. Get spotted. Employers browse a ranked candidate pool, run per-job custom assessments, compare candidates on a common scale, and make pass/fail decisions.
  4. Close the loop. Passed candidates move into the hiring pipeline and are notified; others receive a respectful message with training and alternative-industry suggestions.

The Five-Dimensional Assessment Engine

Knowledge & Expertise

Industry-specific competency, with randomized difficulty-weighted questions and role-prioritized banks.

Personality & Work Style

DOPE Bird / MBTI routing based on role and seniority to capture interpersonal vs. cognitive style.

Cultural Fit

Alignment with team and workplace norms, scored per industry.

Critical Thinking

Scenario-based reasoning and judgment under realistic constraints.

AI Readiness

A 50-question, industry-scoped bank measuring practical fluency with AI tools — the dimension legacy job boards entirely ignore, and the one employers increasingly demand.

Why It Wins

SwiftMatch is not a better job board — it is a different product category. By owning objective measurement, it becomes the trusted source of truth that both candidates and employers anchor on, creating a defensible two-sided network effect.

Why Freemium Works Here

  • Supply first. Free assessments attract candidates, building the verified talent graph that makes employer subscriptions valuable.
  • Natural upgrade triggers. Candidates see a blurred premium report and a clear reason to upgrade; employers hit value the moment they need to compare or shortlist.
  • Compounding moat. Each assessment adds proprietary, structured data that competitors cannot replicate by scraping résumés.

Unit-economics thesis: Candidate acquisition is cheap (free, viral, assessment-driven). Revenue concentrates on the employer side, where willingness-to-pay is highest and a single avoided mis-hire pays for the subscription many times over.

08 · GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY

Go-to-Market Strategy

Phase 1 — Seed the Talent Graph (Months 0–6)

  • Launch free assessments in the Virtual Assistance & BPO verticals where candidate demand for credible verification is highest.
  • Drive candidate acquisition through social proof ("get your verified SwiftMatch score"), community partnerships, and the shareable results report.
  • Goal: a critical mass of verified, scored candidates that makes the employer side immediately valuable.

Phase 2 — Monetize the Employer Side (Months 6–12)

  • Direct sales to remote-staffing agencies, BPOs, and high-volume SMEs.
  • Land-and-expand: free trial of the candidate pool → paid access → custom assessments → placement fees.
  • Activate pass/fail pipeline + automated candidate notifications as a retention and stickiness driver.

Phase 3 — Scale & Defend (Months 12–24)

  • Expand across all 22 industries; introduce the training marketplace and per-hire monetization.
  • Launch mobile app for candidates to maximize reach in a mobile-first market.
  • Build employer-brand and content moat: "SwiftMatch-verified" becomes a recognized signal.

Acquisition Channels

Candidate side

Organic/viral score-sharing, social media, school & community partnerships, SEO on role/industry pages.

Employer side

Founder-led direct sales, staffing-agency partnerships, referral incentives, case studies on reduced time-to-hire.
